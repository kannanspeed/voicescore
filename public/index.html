How We Score

Grammar: We analyze sentence structure, verb tense consistency, article usage, subject-verb agreement, and other grammatical elements.

Fluency: We evaluate speech flow, natural pauses, rhythm, and lack of hesitations or unnecessary fillers.

Confidence: We assess voice strength, clear articulation, and consistent volume throughout your speech.

Pronunciation: We analyze clarity of individual sounds, word stress patterns, and overall intelligibility.

Overall: A comprehensive score based on your performance across all categories, giving you a complete picture of your English speaking skills.
